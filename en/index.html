How it works

Four steps to a report that stands up to the RBQ

  1. Quote within 24 hSend us the address and the number of storeys or levels; we confirm a fixed price in writing.
  2. Site visit and surveyFull visual inspection, then close-up survey of at-risk areas, without closing your building or garage.
  3. Engineer-signed reportPhoto findings, dangerous conditions, recommendations, five-year schedule and budget estimate.
  4. Follow-throughRBQ notice if required, repair tenders and construction oversight.
