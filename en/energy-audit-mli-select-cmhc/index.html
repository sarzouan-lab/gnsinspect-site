MLI Select energy audit: the energy-efficiency points behind your CMHC financing

CMHC’s MLI Select program gives reduced premiums and amortization up to 50 years to rental buildings that earn points for energy efficiency, affordability and accessibility. Our engineers carry out the inspection, the energy model and the audit report your lender files with CMHC.

Request a quote514-965-2842

Which projects

  • Large multi-unit rental buildings governed by Part 3 of the Construction Code.
  • Existing buildings: acquisition, refinancing or renovation.
  • New construction: attestation of performance against the reference code.
  • Owners, developers, investors and mortgage brokers assembling a CMHC file.
The three energy-efficiency levels: for an existing building, a reduction of at least 15%, 25% or 40% in consumption against the baseline earns 20, 35 or 50 points. For new construction the levels are 20%, 25% and 40% better than the reference code. At least 50 points are needed to enter the program, and 100 points for the longest amortization.

What our audit includes

  1. Preliminary review. Bills, drawings and systems: we estimate the achievable point level before you commit fees.
  2. On-site energy inspection. Envelope, heating, ventilation, cooling, domestic hot water, lighting and controls.
  3. Energy modelling. Simulation of the baseline and the improvement scenarios, using methods CMHC recognizes.
  4. Costed scenarios. Cost of each measure, savings and points earned, so you can choose the level to target.
  5. Engineer-signed audit report. The document your lender sends to CMHC.
  6. Support through review. Answers to lender and CMHC questions until approval.

Frequently asked questions

Is the energy audit mandatory for MLI Select?

It is as soon as you claim energy-efficiency points. CMHC requires an energy report and a simulation prepared by a recognized professional, which your lender files with the application.

Can an existing building earn points?

Yes, by demonstrating a reduction of at least 15% in energy consumption against the baseline, through measures completed or planned.

What does September 30, 2026 change for new construction?

New-construction files submitted after that date are assessed against the 2020 codes, which are more demanding. The same building therefore earns fewer points than before; it is worth validating the target level at design stage.

How long does it take?

A few weeks in most cases, depending on building size and the availability of drawings and bills.

Can I combine MLI Select with Hydro-Québec programs?

Yes. They are separate programs: MLI Select concerns financing, Solutions efficaces the cost of the measures. We check what applies to your building.
