Reconstruction value appraisal of your condominium, for the syndicate’s insurance

Since April 15, 2021, the syndicate must have the building’s reconstruction cost appraised at least every 5 years, so the insured amount covers a full rebuild. We carry out the technical survey of the building and coordinate the appraisal with a chartered appraiser, who signs the report the law requires.

What the law requires

  • Every divided co-ownership syndicate must insure the building for its reconstruction value.
  • The reconstruction-cost appraisal must be made by a member of the Ordre des évaluateurs agréés du Québec.
  • It must be redone at least every 5 years.
  • The syndicate must also keep a description of the private portions (reference unit) and a self-insurance fund to pay deductibles.
An appraisal that is too low is expensive on the day of a loss. If the insured amount is below the real rebuilding cost, the gap falls on the co-owners. With construction costs up sharply, an appraisal more than 5 years old is almost always out of date.

Our process

  1. Document review. Declaration of co-ownership, drawings, current insurance policy and last appraisal.
  2. Technical survey by our engineers. Areas, construction systems, envelope, mechanical, electrical, elevators, parking and exterior works.
  3. Common and private portions. Separating what the syndicate must insure from improvements made by co-owners.
  4. Appraisal by a chartered appraiser. New reconstruction cost including demolition, debris removal and professional fees; report signed by the member of the Order.
  5. Delivery to the syndicate and insurer. A report ready to send to your broker.
  6. Reminder before the 5-year deadline. You don’t have to watch the date.

Frequently asked questions

Can an engineer sign the appraisal?

No. The law reserves the reconstruction-cost appraisal for members of the Ordre des évaluateurs agréés du Québec. Our engineers do the technical survey; the chartered appraiser establishes and signs the value.

How does this differ from market value or the municipal assessment?

They are unrelated. Reconstruction value is what it would cost to rebuild the building new today, whatever its market price.

Our last appraisal dates from 2021. Is it still valid?

It expires five years after its date. If it dates from 2021, it must be redone in 2026.

Are co-owners’ improvements included?

No. The syndicate insures the building according to the description of the private portions; each co-owner insures their own improvements.
