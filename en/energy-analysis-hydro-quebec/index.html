Hydro-Québec’s Solutions efficaces program · Energy analysis component

An energy analysis of your building, reimbursed up to $60,000 by Hydro-Québec

If your building uses 1 GWh of electricity or more per year, Hydro-Québec reimburses the cost of an energy analysis carried out by engineers: up to $50,000 for energy efficiency, or $60,000 when the analysis also covers demand response. GNS Inspect, a partner in the program, prepares the application, performs the analysis and stays with you through implementation.

Is your building eligible?

  • Commercial, institutional or industrial building located in Québec and supplied by Hydro-Québec.
  • Electricity use of at least 1 GWh per year (roughly $100,000 or more in annual bills).
  • No energy analysis already submitted to Hydro-Québec for the building in the last five years.
  • The analysis cannot cover lighting only, or aim at electric resistance heating.
How the reimbursement is paid: 40% once Hydro-Québec confirms the analysis report is compliant, then 60% once you implement at least one of the recommended measures. The application must be accepted by Hydro-Québec before the analysis starts.

Our process, from your bills to an implemented measure

  1. Eligibility check, at no charge. Send us 12 months of electricity bills; we confirm the reference consumption and the applicable cap.
  2. Analysis proposal. We prepare the form and the service offer, then file them with Hydro-Québec on your behalf as a partner.
  3. Survey and analysis. Envelope, heating, ventilation, cooling, processes, controls: site measurements, load profiles and costed scenarios.
  4. Engineer-signed analysis report. For each measure: investment, energy and bill savings, payback period, recommendation.
  5. Filing and reimbursement follow-up. Report, invoices and cost grid sent to Hydro-Québec; follow-up through the first payment.
  6. Implementing the measures. Drawings and specifications, the financial-assistance application for the work, oversight and measurement: the step that triggers the second payment.

Frequently asked questions

What does the analysis cost me?

Hydro-Québec reimburses the eligible costs of the analysis up to a cap that depends on the building’s consumption. From 1 to 2 GWh a year: up to $20,000, or $24,000 when the analysis also covers demand response. Over 2 GWh: up to $50,000, or $60,000. You receive 40% when the report is accepted and 60% after implementing at least one measure. We give you a fixed price before filing the application.

What is 1 GWh per year?

One million kWh. Typically a good-sized office or industrial building, a shopping centre, a cold-storage warehouse, an arena or a plant. Your last 12 bills are enough to check.

Do I have to implement every recommended measure?

No. The second payment is triggered by implementing at least one measure that meets program requirements. The rest is at your discretion.

My building uses less than 1 GWh. Is there anything else?

Yes. The program’s Small business and Medium and large business components provide financial assistance for the measures themselves. We check which one applies.

Does Hydro-Québec recommend GNS Inspect?

No. GNS Inspect is a registered partner in the program, which lets us prepare and file applications on your behalf. Hydro-Québec does not recommend or mandate any partner.
