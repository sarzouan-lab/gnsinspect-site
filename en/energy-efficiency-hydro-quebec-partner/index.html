Energy efficiency: partner in Hydro-Québec’s Solutions efficaces program

GNS Inspect is a partner in Hydro-Québec’s Solutions efficaces program. We prepare and file your financial-assistance applications for commercial, institutional and multi-unit buildings: the energy analysis can be reimbursed up to $50,000 ($60,000 when it also covers demand response), and the selected measures partly funded by Hydro-Québec.
