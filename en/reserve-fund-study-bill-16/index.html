Reserve fund study: how much your syndicate must set aside, and when

Since August 2025, every condominium syndicate must obtain a reserve (contingency) fund study, then renew it every 5 years. Existing syndicates have until August 2028 for the first one. Our engineers inspect the building, cost the replacements over 25 years and give you contribution scenarios the assembly can adopt.

What the regulation requires

  • Every divided co-ownership syndicate, whatever its size.
  • First study no later than three years and one day after August 14, 2025, meaning August 2028; then every 5 years.
  • Study based on the maintenance log and a 25-year horizon.
  • Prepared by a member of a recognized professional order with no tie to the co-ownership or its occupants.
  • After the study, the board sets contributions within 30 days of the annual assembly; an underfunded reserve must be replenished within 10 years at most.
Don’t wait for 2028. Eligible professionals will be swamped as the deadline approaches, and an early study spreads contribution increases over more years. Buyers and lenders already ask for these documents.

Our process

  1. Document review. Declaration of co-ownership, drawings, financial statements, work invoices and existing reports.
  2. Building inspection. Roof, envelope, structure, parking, mechanical, electrical and exterior works in the common portions.
  3. Component inventory. Condition, remaining service life and replacement cost of each element.
  4. 25-year projection. Schedule of major work and the fund’s cash flow, year by year.
  5. Contribution scenarios. Two or three options, from fast catch-up to spreading over 10 years.
  6. Presentation to the assembly. We explain the findings to co-owners and answer their questions.

Frequently asked questions

Is our small 6-unit building covered?

Yes. The regulation applies to every syndicate. Small co-ownerships only benefit from a maintenance-log review every 10 years instead of every 5.

Do we need the maintenance log before the study?

The study relies on the log. We produce both together, which costs less than two separate mandates.

What if the fund is insufficient?

The board must adjust annual contributions and has at most 10 years to replenish the fund. Our scenarios show the effect on each fraction.

Who can sign the study?

A member of the orders of engineers, architects, professional technologists, chartered appraisers or CPAs, independent of the co-ownership.

How long does it take?

Four to eight weeks depending on building size and document availability.
