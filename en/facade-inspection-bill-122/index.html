Bill 122 · Safety Code, Building Chapter · s. 371–384

Bill 122 façade inspection: an engineer-signed report that satisfies the RBQ

If your building has 5 or more storeys above grade, the law requires an engineer or architect to verify its façades every 5 years. GNS Inspect delivers the verification report, signed by an OIQ-licensed engineer, with a prioritized, costed maintenance plan.

Who must comply

  • Any building of 5+ storeys above grade: residential, condominium, commercial or institutional.
  • First verification within 10 years of construction, then every 5 years.
  • The owner must keep a register; the report must be produced to the RBQ on request.
  • Any dangerous condition must be corrected without delay and reported to the RBQ.
Not sure when your next deadline falls? Send us the year of construction and the date of the last report: we will confirm the deadline at no charge.

What our verification includes

  1. Document review. Drawings, prior reports, maintenance register, completed work.
  2. Full visual inspection. All elevations from street level, roof and balconies.
  3. Close-up inspection. Lift, rope access or drone depending on access: masonry ties, lintels, expansion joints, parapets, balconies, sills.
  4. Exploratory openings where warranted. Openings, borescope, tie pull tests, moisture surveys.
  5. Safety-Code-compliant report. Photo findings, dangerous-condition classification, recommendations, schedule and budget estimate.
  6. Follow-through. RBQ dangerous-condition notice if required, repair specifications, construction oversight.

Frequently asked questions

My building has 4 storeys plus a basement. Am I covered?

No. The threshold is 5 storeys above grade. A basement or underground garage does not count as an above-grade storey.

Is an architect’s report equivalent?

Yes, the law accepts both professions. An engineer is recommended where structure, masonry ties or balconies are involved.

What happens if a dangerous condition is found?

The owner must immediately protect the public and notify the RBQ. We prepare the notice, the protection perimeter and the correction plan.

How long does the inspection take?

Half a day to two days depending on building size and access method (lift, rope access or drone).

How much does a Bill 122 façade inspection cost?

Price depends on the number of elevations, height and access method. We confirm a fixed price in writing within 24 hours, no hidden fees.
