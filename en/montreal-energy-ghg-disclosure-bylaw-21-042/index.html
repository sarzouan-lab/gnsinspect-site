Annual energy and GHG disclosure: we handle it for you

In Montréal, owners of buildings of 2,000 m² or more, or with 25 or more dwellings, must report the building’s energy consumption to the City every year by June 30. We gather the data, set up your file in ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager and submit it on time.

Request a quote514-965-2842

Who is covered

  • Commercial and institutional buildings of 2,000 m² (21,528 sq ft) or more.
  • Residential buildings with 25 or more dwellings.
  • The report covers monthly consumption for the whole building, from January 1 to December 31 of the previous year.
  • Some industrial-use buildings are excluded; we check your situation.
An obligation that comes back every year. The City compares buildings and assigns each a rating; performance thresholds are planned under the Climate Plan. A well-built report is also the starting point for an energy analysis reimbursed by Hydro-Québec.

Our turnkey service

  1. Applicability check. Floor area, number of dwellings and use: we confirm whether your building is covered.
  2. Data collection. Transfer of Hydro-Québec and Énergir data, and other energy sources, for every meter in the building.
  3. ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager file. Creation or update of the building profile, validation of areas and uses.
  4. Quality control by an engineer. Missing meters, incomplete months and abnormal variances caught before submission.
  5. Submission to the City before June 30. With written confirmation of filing.
  6. Annual summary. Your energy intensity, emissions and improvement leads, on one page.

Why hand it to us

  • You no longer have to think about it: a reminder every spring, filing before the deadline.
  • Engineers read your data, not just a form.
  • If your building uses 1 GWh or more a year, we check at the same time whether it qualifies for the energy analysis reimbursed by Hydro-Québec.
  • Fixed annual price per building, portfolio rate for several buildings.

Frequently asked questions

Is my 30-unit building covered?

Yes. The by-law applies to residential buildings with 25 or more dwellings, and to buildings of 2,000 m² or more.

What is the deadline?

June 30 of each year, for the previous calendar year’s consumption.

I have no access to my tenants’ meters. What do I do?

The report covers whole-building consumption. Energy distributors offer an aggregated data transfer; we take care of the request.

What does the City do with the data?

It compares buildings and assigns each a GHG emissions rating. Performance thresholds have been announced for the coming years.

How does this relate to Hydro-Québec’s energy analysis?

The same data shows whether your building reaches 1 GWh a year, the threshold for an analysis reimbursed up to $60,000.
